About

Your Event ProductionPartner in Portugal.

BILOBA is a Lisbon-based event agency specializing in corporate events, brand experiences and private celebrations.

We combine creative direction, comprehensive event management and technical production to deliver projects across Portugal.

From finding the right venue and selecting trusted local suppliers to managing budgets, timelines, scenography, logistics and on-site execution, our team takes responsibility for the entire process.

We provide international clients with the local expertise, clear communication and professional oversight required to deliver events with confidence.

Expertise

One Team.Complete Event Management.

Our services cover every stage of event planning and production, providing a single point of coordination throughout the project.

  • Event Planning & Project Management

    Brief development, venue sourcing, budget management, supplier selection and booking, contract coordination, detailed timelines and production schedules.

  • Creative Direction & Scenography

    Concept development, spatial design, set design, furniture, lighting, visual installations and the overall creative environment.

  • Technical Production & Logistics

    Temporary structures, audiovisual production, staging, lighting, power, technical infrastructure, transport, installation and dismantling.

  • On-Site Event Management

    Production supervision, supplier coordination, technical rehearsals, event operations and complete on-site delivery.
